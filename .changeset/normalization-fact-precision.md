Normalization facts are business normalization (FC-4b). Many `normalization` facts were not. Every tier of the C#, TypeScript, Java and Go plugins now reports the nearest call that receives a normalized value (its consumer, up to the statement and never past a lambda), with its receiver and declaring types in the full tiers, and the shared finisher drops the fact when that consumer is a message sink: a logging method on a known logger type (Microsoft.Extensions.Logging, Serilog, NLog, log4net, `System.Diagnostics.Debug`/`Trace`, java.util.logging, SLF4J, log4j, commons-logging, Go `log`, `log/slog`, logrus, zap, zerolog, and in TypeScript `Console`, pino, winston, NestJS `Logger`/`LoggerService`, bunyan, log4js, tslog), the same methods on a logger-named receiver in every tier whenever no known logger type resolves, or an exception or error construction (`new ...Exception`, `new Error`, `errors.New`, `fmt.Errorf`). `auth.LogIn` or `writer.WriteLine` are no sinks, and `log.info("saved", repo.upsert(email.toLowerCase()))` keeps its fact. Display text (an interpolation hole or `+` operand next to text with whitespace) is dropped only when it goes to a sink or a formatting API (`StringBuilder.Append*`, `string.Format`, `fmt.Sprintf`, `Console.Write*`); SQL templates and space-joined keys keep their facts. Calls on formats (`Path` and character calls, hex or base64 output, file extensions, separator-only trims) give none, and a chain is one fact, the outermost call, also across lines. `ctxo index` and `ctxo watch` drop `normalization` facts in test files, as they drop `retry-timeout` facts; the `@ctxo/domain` text scan applies the sink rule to the call enclosing a normalization on its line. The `call` evidence gains `consumer`, `consumerTypes` and `inProse`, with new conformance vectors `isMessageSink`, `isDisplayCall`, `isTechnicalNormalization` and `feedsNormalization`. The Java full tier needs `@ctxo/lang-java-analyzer` at this release (run `ctxo update`); an older JAR reports no consumers, so only the format, chain and test-file rules apply there. A `shadow-specs.yaml` resolution recorded for a dropped call, such as the inner `email.Trim()` of a chain, turns stale (check 12 warns) and can be removed.
